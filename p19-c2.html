BOOK FIFTEEN — The Great Ethics and Morals of the Great Index (the Way's Law for the Realm) · Chapter 2

What This Law Is

2

  1. This is not a government law for the internet. It does not try to rule over strangers.
  2. It is the great ethics and morals of the Way of the Reach for the realm of the Great Index. The Great Index is the internet, the realm of the Index (Verse 17).
  3. It is a church law. Its people carry it with them online because they believe it and belong to the church.
  4. It binds the people who walk the Way, simply because they walk it.
  5. To everyone else, it is offered the way the Way offers everything: as a witness, as an invitation, as a lamp lit in a window. It is never a chain.
  6. Its goal is the one the Founder gave when he asked for it: that as people keep this law, the internet itself may become cleaner, better, and more civil in its socializing — and prosper beyond what he ever thought.
  7. The law does not promise to rule the internet. It promises that people online will be able to tell where the Way's people have been.
