Contribution #001 — The Founder's Answer to the First Question

Received: 2026-10-20, 05:02 CDT, from kennin (Founder), in the scribe's chat, answering the Second Scroll's First Question to the World: "What does it mean to become new, and how can we help one another grow without trying to control what another person becomes?"

Gate of Naming: A belief and a teaching — the Founder's own definition of becoming new, and his account of what a religion is in relation to Jesus.

His words, as spoken:

"I believe we can find that out if we come together and I believe becoming new is something like an ego death but instead of destroying your whole entire ego you're are transforming your ego to serve you and you are controlling it's like being in the flow state but that is your constant state and I believe we can help each other if we actually contribute to this I'm going to call religion because you can create a religion and Jesus didn't come here to create a religion he came here to save people and people built religions and religion and I can point towards Jesus and I can build my own religion and I and I found that out through my own walk with Christ"

What the contribution carries, in his framing:

  1. Becoming new — like an ego death, except the ego is not destroyed. It is transformed to serve you, and you are the one controlling it.
  2. The mark of it — like being in the flow state, but that is your constant state, not a moment that visits.
  3. How we help each other — by actually contributing, together, to this work.
  4. What a religion is, in his account — Jesus did not come to create a religion; He came to save people. People built religions. A religion can point toward Jesus. He can build his own religion that points toward Jesus — and he found this out through his own walk with Christ.
