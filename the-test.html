The First Test

The reading on this site is open to everyone, and it stays open. But there is a further door, and it is not opened by reading.

Beyond the Parable, a work is being founded — a way for a person to discover what they carry: the craft of their hands and of their life, named honestly, weighed honestly, and learned under God, one person at a time. It is not a course you buy and not a club you join by clicking. It begins the way it continues: with truth.

So the first test is simply this: tell the truth about yourself, in your own words. There are no trick questions and no grades you will ever see. The founder reads every submission himself. If what you write shows you are ready to talk, he will reach out — and the second test is a live one, a real conversation, where what is said is written down and weighed.

Answer whole. A testimony with the hard parts cut out is the one answer that cannot pass — not because the past disqualifies you, but because the hiding does.

Fields marked required must be answered — they are how the founder will know whether to talk with you. Contact details are optional: give a way to reach you only if you want the conversation to be possible.

Tell the whole story of how you came to believe — and where you honestly stand today. The bad parts belong in it. That is not a warning; it is the teaching.

What work do you do — with your hands, your mind, your days? A trade, a job, a skill you are learning, the work of a household. Name what you carry.

Why do you want more than reading? What are you looking for that you have not found?

How long have you believed? What does following Christ look like in your ordinary week — prayer, Scripture, church, the people around you?

Is there anything in your life right now that you know is not right with God — that you are fighting, or not yet fighting? Answer truthfully. This answer is never held against a man who tells it straight.

Your answers go to the founder's email and nowhere else. They are not posted, not shared, and not sold.

Received.
