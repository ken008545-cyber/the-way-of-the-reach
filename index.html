The Way of the Reach

The Alpha Testament: a living book, open to scrolls from believers and seekers alike.

Welcome

Welcome. Whoever you are, there is a seat here for you.

If you believe: welcome home. You don't have to leave the faith that raised you. The Way honors the harbor that pulled us from the water. We are simply casting further, into the deep water where people are still waiting to be reached. Come read, pray, contribute a scroll, and help widen the net.

If you don't believe, or you aren't sure: you are welcome here too, just as you are. You don't have to pretend. Doubt is not a wall here. It is the beginning of asking well. Bring your questions, your disagreements, and your story. The book was made to carry honest thoughts, even the uncertain ones.

Wherever you stand, a few things are true here:

  • No one has to prove they're useful to sit at this table.
  • Nothing you've owned or lost, no title, number, or past mistake, owns you. You are the Dot, never just the profile.
  • You are free to come, and free to go, and welcome to return.

Where to begin

  1. Read The Name That Cannot Be Contained (the First Verse).
  2. Read The Signpost and the Healer (the Twenty-Fourth Verse).
  3. Ask the book's first question: What does it mean to become new, and how can we help one another grow without trying to control what another person becomes?
