THE SEALED GATE

Beyond this gate stands the Yeshua craftsmanship — the foundation of a work being built under God. It is not hidden out of shame; it is sealed because it is discovered, by those who are given to enter.

Twelve keys open this gate — one for each of the twelve tribes, each bearing its tribe's symbol. Whichever tribe's key you hold, the gate is the same. Enter your key whole, exactly as it was given. It opens here, in your browser only, for this visit. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, or kept.

"But thou, O Daniel, shut up the words, and seal the book, even to the time of the end." — Daniel 12:4
